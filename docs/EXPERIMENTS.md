# Experiments and extreme environments

Every phenotype must be interpretable in context. Therefore environment and challenge are first-class records.

Example challenge:

```text
Arid Water Efficiency
- high daytime temperature
- low relative humidity
- 55% control water allocation
Selection goals:
- water-use efficiency
- growth retention
- recovery after stress
```

A cohort comparison is descriptive until replication/statistical design supports a stronger conclusion.

The same contract can describe:
- home grow comparisons
- greenhouse breeding trials
- desert/heat trials
- salinity trials
- constrained-energy growth
- closed-loop habitat experiments
- future orbital/lunar/planetary controlled-environment studies

Conditions that cannot be faithfully simulated on Earth must remain explicitly labeled as simulated proxies.
